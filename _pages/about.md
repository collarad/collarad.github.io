I am a Team Lead and Senior Research Engineer at Fraunhofer FIT, where I develop Neurosymbolic AI systems that combine symbolic reasoning and factual grounding with generative AI to create robust, enterprise-grade solutions that address LLM limitations.



At Fraunhofer, I embrace and refine the research-driven approach to innovation, ensuring each design decision is guided by a clear research question and leads to market-oriented solutions. My work includes: (1) Developing enterprise knowledge graphs as a semantic layer for LLMs and Agentic AI; (2) Deep learning on Graphs, with focus on creating scalable and time-aware Graph Foundation Models (GFM) to enhance predictive AI for enterprise data; (3)  employing KGs as agentic memory for continuous learning in LLM-based agents; (4) improving knowledge-enhanced generation with GraphRAG methods to reduce hallucinations. (5) establishing best practices and fine-tuning methods for open-weights LLMs; and (6) applying mechanistic interpretability to identify steering vectors to enable real-time monitoring and flag harmful internal states in LLMs.



I lead the conceptualization, design, and development of AI conversational assistants and agentic systems for automation. My neuro-symbolic approaches enhance factual accuracy, reduce hallucinations, and adapt to changing business requirements. I hold a Master's degree in Software Engineering and a Ph.D., providing expertise in project management, software architecture, and complex AI problem-solving. In addition to strong publications on these topics, I contributed to the development of DIN SPEC 91526:2025-05 for integrating knowledge graphs and LLMs.



Beyond technical expertise, I believe inclusive, purpose-driven leadership is essential for fostering innovation. I mentor four PhD students and coach young AI talent in Bolivia, which contributed to receiving the Google AI Scholar Award in 2020.
